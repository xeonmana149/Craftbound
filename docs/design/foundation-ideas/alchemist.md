# Alchemist — Foundation concepts

**Hero power — Throw Potion, 2 XP.** Deal 1 damage to a mob, block or hero. It is a hero power, so it does not trigger “after you play an Action.” It spends a real turn-two resource to finish something off.

Alchemist wants a board full of *wounded* bodies. A cheap splash sets up the precise last hit. Its Undead are mostly expendable delivery mobs that leave ingredients or a last dose behind. The class pays for its flexibility with below-rate attackers, symmetrical sweeps and awkward turns when the opponent plays one large threat. Some designs use **Dose**, a proposed payoff for playing a second Action in the same turn. Start testing the ordinary splash package before turning it on.

**Curve.** 1 XP × 6 · 2 XP × 9 · 3 XP × 10 · 4 XP × 6 · 5 XP × 3 · 6 XP × 2 · 7 XP × 2 · 8 XP × 1 · 9 XP × 1.

**Rarity mix.** 18 Common, 14 Rare, 6 Epic, 2 Legendary. Stats are Attack/Health for mobs, Health for blocks, and Attack/Durability for tools. **Ready** uses current Workshop rules. **Pair** also needs another named proposal in this chapter. **New** needs the implementation described after the table.

**How it plays.** On turn three, trade with Spider-Eye Forager and aim its Bottled Prickle at a survivor. If two enemy mobs are down to one Health, Spill the Cauldron clears them both, including your own Forager if it is still alive. Against a single target, Stray Apothecary buys a turn without paying for a sweep. Against blocks, hold Marsh Distillate or Soulfire Distiller.

**Two builds to try.** An Undead list can run Bone Ash, Bottling Skeleton, Calcium Condenser and Brewkeeper’s Familiar. Drawing for two Undead makes the Familiar a real payoff without demanding a full row. A potion list can bank Frosted Vial and Bottled Prickle for a three-XP Dose turn. At 9 XP, Mirelda gives those decks a sequence: play her, play both Prickles, then resolve her splash. A saved 2-Health friendly mob dies in the same splash.

**Implementation boundaries.** Dose means “After you play your second Action this turn.” Count plays by the owner, including the second Action even if the listener entered play after the first. It fires once per owner turn, after that Action resolves. Double-Necked Retort would also need the played Action’s identity and a copy-to-hand effect. Bitter Antidote needs a type-conditioned replacement. Ordinary healing in the current runtime heals Undead too, so the exception belongs on this card. Wither damages a mob at the start of its owner’s turn. Actions, Sweep damage and hero-power damage are separate events.

**First balance check.** Against six enemies at 2 Health, Spore Splash is a strong clear that also kills friendly support and damages blocks. If the three-XP price crowds out every other answer, test it at four. Track how many cards Ashen Mortar converts into damage before opponents can reach its back row. The Crucible is expensive and occupies a block slot, but repeated Skeleton deaths can still draw through a deck quickly.
