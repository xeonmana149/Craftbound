# Builder — Give the walls something to protect

**Build** costs 2 XP and summons a 0/2 Cobblestone Wall. At the end of your turn it gives a random friendly mob +1 Health. A wall by itself can’t hold off an attacker. A Wall behind a Copper Sentinel keeps a Shield mob alive for another trade. With only three block slots, the third structure should earn its place.

This pool supports a Golem formation that trades repeatedly and a slower workshop that turns blocks into cards and reinforcements. Both need mobs in front. **Ready** uses existing Workshop effects. **Pair** also needs another candidate from this chapter. **New** names a rule the engine would need. Mob stats are Attack/Health and tool stats are Attack/Durability.

## How the decks play

**Golem formation.** Play Copper Sentinel on turn 3. On turn 4, trade into a 2-Attack mob and use Build afterward. If the Sentinel is your only mob, it gets the Wall’s Health buff. Buttress Golem lets it trade up on the next turn. Neutral Iron Golem is another body for those buffs. Removing the Buttress immediately removes its Attack aura.

**Workshop economy.** Falsework needs two open block slots to produce both pieces. If it survives to your next turn, it replaces itself with a draw and leaves the Wall behind. Later, use Site Surveyor before Hoist Operator. With three blocks you draw three, then pick which collectible block to take back. Returning a token Wall removes it from play instead. A full back row makes another Build impossible until you clear space.

**Closing the game.** Ironwork Assembly refills an empty mob row. The Walking Keep rewards Golems that survived the previous turn. It only buffs other Golems already in play, so it’s much weaker after a board clear. Hesta offers a third approach if implemented. Bank Health on one block, then cash it in for one large attacker. Like any new mob without Sprint, that Golem gives the opponent a turn to respond.

**Counterplay and first tests.** Pressure early, send Ranged attacks into the back row when there is no defending Shield mob, or save direct block removal for Bell Foundry. Check how often Wall-only opening hands can contest turn 3, whether an uncontested Foundry fills both the board and hand too easily, and how many cards Site Surveyor actually draws. If the Foundry snowballs, test a start-of-turn summon so the opponent can answer the first Courier.

**First build.** Implement Copper Sentinel, Cobble Courier, Site Surveyor, Hoist Operator and Ironwork Assembly together. That small group tests front-row protection, the value of a third structure, a way to make space and a payoff for keeping the workshop running.
