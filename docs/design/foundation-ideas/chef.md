# Chef — Keep a table full of Villagers

**Feed** costs 2 XP and restores 2 Health to a mob, block, or either hero. Ordinary healing stops at maximum Health. A “when healed” effect needs at least one point of real healing, so Feed on a full-health Villager cannot farm triggers. The current engine heals Undead normally. None of these ideas assumes that healing damages them.

Chef wants one unit sturdy enough to trade twice and Villagers worth saving behind it. That makes an opponent’s choices matter: damage spread across the board makes a feast efficient, while a clean kill gives Chef nothing to heal. **Ready** uses existing Workshop rules. **Pair** uses another card proposed here, and **New** needs extra support. Mob stats are Attack/Health, tool stats are Attack/Durability, and Blocks list Health.

### Two decks to try

**One more trade.** Play Baker’s Apprentice on turn two. If it lives through a trade, turn three can be Feed plus Pantry Loaf, first repairing damage and then adding maximum Health. If the Apprentice is already full, keep the heal and consider developing Cookpot Hearth instead. Put **Shield** on the unit you can afford to lose; the Hearth earns no Attack buff on a dead defender.

**Market kitchen.** Establish two Villagers, then use Covered Pot or Ladle Polish to heal several injured units. Cookpot Hearth rewards each Villager that actually gains Health. Market Day gives you two fresh cards and can trigger the Hearth for each injured Villager. Drawing into a full ten-card hand burns the overflow. The late choice is Bruna to multiply future healing or Edric to produce an immediate blocker and recover hero Health. Iron Golem needs an open mob slot after Edric enters.

**First playtest.** Track *real Health restored* and how often excess healing from Sweet Roll becomes permanent growth. A heal for 3 on a mob missing 1 gives it 1 ordinary healing and 2 extra maximum Health through **Well Fed**. A Health stat buff is a separate event and does not trigger Cookpot Hearth or Bruna. Chef should be vulnerable to effects that destroy, return, or Cleanse a single heavily buffed mob; its recurring engines should take time to answer a wide enemy board.
