# Fisherman — Foundation concepts

**Hero power — What a Catch!, 2 XP.** Summon a random **Cod** (1/3), **Salmon** (2/1) or **Pufferfish** (1/1). All three are Aquatic. When Pufferfish dies to an enemy mob, that killer loses 2 Attack through the end of its controller’s next turn. Catching a fish places it on the board; it does not trigger **Schooling**, which specifically watches Aquatic mobs played from hand.

The Fisherman deck has two jobs: put bodies in the water, then decide which fish to keep and which to trade. A small catch makes a good buff target or buys a turn for a fragile Schooling attacker. Rods and return-to-hand effects make a second, slower “fill the creel” build possible. Its reach against blocks comes from Ranged mobs; it has little direct removal and few answers to wide boards.

**Curve.** 1 XP × 5 · 2 XP × 10 · 3 XP × 9 · 4 XP × 7 · 5 XP × 3 · 6 XP × 2 · 7 XP × 2 · 8 XP × 1 · 9 XP × 1.

**Rarity mix.** 18 Common, 14 Rare, 6 Epic, 2 Legendary. Stats are Attack/Health for mobs, Health for blocks, and Attack/Durability for tools. **Ready** uses current Workshop rules. **Pair** also needs another named proposal in this chapter. **New** needs the implementation described after the table. The fish tokens from What a Catch! are already available.

**How it plays.** What a Catch! on turn two gives material for Tangle-Kelp Knot even when the roll is a Pufferfish. For a burst turn, play Schooling Squid before Riverbank Fisher. The Fisher adds a zero-XP Cod, and playing the Cod gives the Squid +2 Attack for the turn. The Fisher is a Villager and does not trigger Schooling himself. A Cod summoned by the hero power also leaves the Squid at base Attack.

**Two builds to try.** Board Fisherman curves Reef Darter into fish and keeps one or two spaces open for Herring Tide. With two spaces left, the third Cod cannot arrive. Creel Fisherman instead trades with Reed Fishing Rod while Keeper of the Creel turns incoming fish into buffs. At nine cards in hand, avoid Creel Basket unless burning the second Salmon is acceptable. Opponents can kill the Keeper before the Rod’s next swing.

**Implementation boundaries.** **Haul** means “the first time each turn you add a card to your hand without drawing it.” Current hand-added effects count draws as well as generated cards and need a source and once-per-turn flag to implement Haul. Returning a collectible mob already calls the give-to-hand routine and should count as a Haul. A full hand never counts as a successful addition. Prismarine Harpoon needs to check that the tool hit a block before it adds a catch. A Ranged tool can already reach the back row while ordinary enemy mobs are present. Blue-Hole Whirlpool needs the number of successful returns per owner and a way to summon replacement fish for that owner. Marina’s granting line also needs to resolve the Haul trigger for her own generated gifts. It fires only once across both gifts.

**First balance check.** The hero-power fish cost 0 when added to hand, so repeated additions can fuel Schooling with almost no XP. Count how often Schooling Squid attacks for 5 or more on turns three and four. If Keeper of the Creel produces too many permanent stats, cap that listener at once each turn before cutting the fish generation players built around. Blue-Hole Whirlpool is the experimental top-end slot; test it separately because bouncing into a full enemy hand may remove the excess mobs permanently.
