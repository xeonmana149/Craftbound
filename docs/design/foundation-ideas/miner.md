# Miner — Spend the vein or leave it standing?

**Prospect · 2 XP, once each turn.** Add an Iron Ore to your hand. It is a **1-XP Action** that gives a friendly mob +1/+1. It is not an Ore block and never counts as mining. Even without a vein in play, the power can help a surviving mob trade up.

**Two ways to build.** A **quarry deck** uses cheap Villagers and tools to open veins, then spends the proceeds on Golems. A **deep-delving deck** keeps one or two veins standing to power survey cards, then cashes them in when it needs the space. An Ore block pays its **Drop** whether the opponent destroys it or you **Mine** it. Only deliberate mining triggers your crew's “after you mine” effects. That difference gives the opponent a reason to break an Ore early.

**Read the table.** Mob stats are Attack/Health, tool stats Attack/Durability, and blocks show Health only. “Workshop” uses existing operations; linked cards still need definitions if you select those ideas. “Prototype” names the missing operation. All numbers are proposals.

## At the table

- **A real turn-three decision:** With Lapis Vein and Lamp Carrier in play, spend 1 XP on Loose Shale. Lapis draws one card, Loose Shale draws another, and the Carrier gains an Attack. Or keep the vein and play Tunnel Brace so Ranged attackers have to hit its Shield first.
- **A combat trick without a spell-speed system:** Swing with Stone Pickaxe while Copper Pocket is your only Ore. Its Drop gives a surviving friendly mob +2 Health before you attack with that mob. Attack order matters.
- **A reason to leave resources alone:** Survey Journal wants veins standing; Minecart Junction wants them spent. Atlas rewards saving your last vein for turn nine: its own excavation can immediately give you a 2/5 Shield defender if there’s space. Don't try to fit three engines into three block slots. If the opponent owns a Ranged mob, a stocked quarry can evaporate before your next turn.
- **A starter 30 to test:** Take two each of #01, 02, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 11, 12, 17, 20, 22, 26, 29 and 36. Replace the two expensive payoffs with neutral bodies if this trades too slowly. No Legendary or prototype is needed.
- **Counterplay:** This class spends early turns building instead of attacking. A fast front row can punish that, while Cleanse on a boosted Golem punishes investing every Iron Ore in one mob. A Totem on an Ore is insurance against destruction; deliberately mining it bypasses the Totem. Silk Touch returns a vein without its Drop.
- **One keyword to test later — Vein:** “After you deliberately mine this Ore, [effect].” Opponent destruction would still give the printed Drop, but not Vein. Use it only if testing shows players enjoy choosing between the two payouts; ordinary Mine and Drop already make a deck.
